Our Story

Emerald Escapes was founded in Galway in 2015 by two friends who spent their weekends exploring the quiet corners of Ireland that most visitors never see. What started as trips for family and friends grew into a travel company built on local knowledge, small groups and genuine Irish hospitality.

We plan holidays for couples looking for a short break, families wanting adventures everyone can enjoy, and visitors from abroad hoping to experience Ireland the way locals do. Whether it's a weekend in the city or a week on the Wild Atlantic Way, we take care of the details so you can enjoy the journey.
